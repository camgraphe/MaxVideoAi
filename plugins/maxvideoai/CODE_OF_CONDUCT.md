# Code of Conduct

MaxVideoAI adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as the behavior standard for this project. The concise project guidance below explains how that standard applies here; the linked text is authoritative.

## What behavior belongs here?

Be respectful, specific, and constructive. Welcome different levels of experience, assume good intent while addressing real impact, and keep technical disagreement focused on the work. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and retaliation are not acceptable.

**Example**: Critique the proposed documentation or reproduction steps; do not attack the contributor.

## Where does this scope apply?

The standard covers repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, release work, and other project spaces. It also applies when someone officially represents MaxVideoAI in a public or private community setting.

## How do I report conduct privately?

Do not post a conduct report publicly. Email [support@maxvideoai.com](mailto:support@maxvideoai.com) with the subject `Private conduct report`. Include the relevant context, approximate time, and impact, but do not include unrelated credentials, private customer media, or payment data.

```text
Subject: Private conduct report
Channel: support@maxvideoai.com
Visibility: private
```

Security vulnerabilities follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

## How is the standard enforced?

Project maintainers may clarify expectations, edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban a participant according to the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines. Reports are reviewed as privately as practical, with attention to reporter safety, conflicts of interest, context, and proportional action.
