# Compare AI video models without spending first

**Short answer:** Ask MaxVideoAI for current model options and comparable named budgets before you prepare a paid request. The useful decision is the route that fits this shot’s quality, control, duration, reference, and cost trade-offs—not a permanent winner.

![MaxVideoAI workspace with the Luma Ray 2 Flash selector and a completed glass-ribbon result](../assets/demos/model-choice-and-budget.webp)

*This current MaxVideoAI product visual proves a model selector and completed result. It does not prove a budget, exact quote, approval, model ranking, or native ChatGPT, Claude, or Codex execution.*

## What are you deciding?

Choose a route for a concrete brief, then identify the trade-off it accepts. Current availability and supported settings depend on the live MaxVideoAI model facts and the selected workflow.

**Prompt to copy:** “Use MaxVideoAI to compare current executable models for a 20-second product film with three shots. Give me a quality-first route, a control-first route, and a lower-cost route with named project budgets. Explain the trade-off for each, and do not prepare or approve a generation.”

## What MaxVideoAI should do

`$plan` should turn the brief into current options and comparable named budgets without spending credits. Treat the project budget as a decision aid, not an exact quote, reservation, or debit.

When one route is ready, use `$generate` to prepare the selected concrete shot. It should return a fresh exact quote. Only explicit approval authorizes one paid attempt.

```text
Compare named routes → select one concrete shot → receive its exact quote → approve one paid attempt
```

## What to do if the conversation stops

If a request was already approved and submitted, check the accepted job or recent generations before preparing another request. Recover the completed result or refunded outcome through the MaxVideoAI Library; a replacement needs a fresh exact quote and new explicit approval.

Read [how the workflow divides responsibility](../docs/how-it-works.md), then compare the [current public model information](https://maxvideoai.com/models). Bring the brief.
