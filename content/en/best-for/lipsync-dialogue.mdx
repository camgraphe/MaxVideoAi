## Dialogue prompting checklist
- Keep one or two short sentences per beat.
- Keep the face visible and the camera movement restrained.
- Give every speaker a stable visual and voice reference.
- Separate dialogue, ambience, and sound-effect instructions.
- Use a new beat when the speaker, camera, or action changes.

## FAQ
### What is the best AI video model for lip sync?
Seedance 2.5 is the strongest all-around option. Happy Horse 1.1 is the focused specialist when lip sync is the main requirement.

### Which model is best for several speaking characters?
Use Seedance 2.5 for longer referenced dialogue or Kling O3 Pro for a tightly planned sequence.

### How long should each spoken line be?
Short lines are safer. Break a long script into separate beats so facial motion and timing remain believable.
