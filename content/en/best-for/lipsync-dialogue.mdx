## Your scene comes first
The line only works if you believe the person saying it. Listen first, then check the mouth, expression and timing.

## The shortlist, in detail
1. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** — Generate sound with a 5–15 second scene, or use audio references to guide it. Keep the line clear and review the voice, words and mouth together.
2. **[Google Veo 3.1](/models/veo-3-1)** — Explore text, image and reference workflows, including first-and-last-frame control and 4K output options.
3. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** — Build a scene up to 30 seconds, starting with text, an image or a mix of visual and audio references.
4. **[Happy Horse 1.1](/models/happy-horse-1-1)** — Create from text, an image or references with audio generated alongside the video, up to 1080p.
5. **[LTX 2.5 Pro](/models/ltx-2-5-pro)** — Use text or an image, or choose audio-to-video with an owned audio file to guide a 1080p scene.

## Questions before you create
### What is the best AI video model for lip sync?
Start with H3 for a short scene with generated dialogue, or Veo 3.1 when frame control matters. Seedance 2.5 gives longer exchanges more room; HappyHorse 1.1 offers another sound-led option. Choose LTX 2.5 Pro when you already have the audio. Listen and check mouth timing before delivery.

### Which model is best for several speaking characters?
Use Seedance 2.5 for longer referenced dialogue or Kling O3 Pro for a tightly planned sequence.

### How long should each spoken line be?
Short lines are safer. Break a long script into separate beats so facial motion and timing remain believable.
