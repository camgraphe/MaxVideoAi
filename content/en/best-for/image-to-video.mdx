## What you are optimizing for
Image-to-video should preserve the approved frame while adding believable motion. The strongest current models also let you guide the ending, reuse subjects, add audio, or combine the image with a wider reference pack.

## Best picks
1. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** - best overall for start and end frames, long cinematic motion, multimodal references, and native audio.
2. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** - best for high-detail image animation up to 4K with native stereo audio and optional end-frame control.
3. **[Grok Imagine Video 1.5](/models/grok-imagine-video-1-5)** - best for fast, convincing motion and physics from a starting image, with synchronized audio.
4. **[Wan 3 Prime](/models/wan-3-prime)** - best premium Wan route for first/last-frame and reference-led image workflows.
5. **[Kling O3 Pro](/models/kling-o3-pro)** - best when the image belongs to a controlled character, product, or storyboard workflow.

## Why these models lead now
Seedance 2.5 combines image starts, end frames, up to 30 seconds, and large multimodal reference packs. MiniMax H3 is the sharper delivery pick when resolution and native stereo sound matter. Grok Imagine Video 1.5 is a practical fast route for a single approved image, while Wan 3 Prime and Kling O3 Pro add stronger reference and sequence control.

## Choose by workflow
- Use Seedance 2.5 for a longer campaign or story built from approved frames.
- Use MiniMax H3 when the image must survive motion at high resolution.
- Use Grok Imagine Video 1.5 for fast image-led social and cinematic tests.
- Use Wan 3 Prime or Kling O3 Pro when several references must stay coherent.

## FAQ
### What is the best image-to-video model?
Seedance 2.5 is the strongest general starting point. Choose MiniMax H3 for 4K delivery or Grok Imagine Video 1.5 for a faster single-image workflow.

### Should I use an end frame?
Use one when the landing composition matters. A strong start frame plus a clear end frame usually gives the model a cleaner motion target.

### How do I reduce subject drift?
Keep the action simple, state what must remain unchanged, and use reference-capable models when identity or product geometry matters.
