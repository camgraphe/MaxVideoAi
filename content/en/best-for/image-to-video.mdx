## Your scene comes first
You have the image. Now decide what should move — and what must stay exactly where it is.

## The shortlist, in detail
1. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** — Start with your opening image and direct a scene up to 30 seconds. Describe what moves and what must stay recognisable.
2. **[Kling 3.0 Omni Pro](/models/kling-o3-pro)** — A 1080p option for image-led scenes, reference-guided work and video transformation. Plan what each reference should control.
3. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** — Generate 5–15 second clips with audio, with output options up to 4K and support for image, video and audio references.
4. **[FLUX 3](/models/flux-3)** — Use a first and last frame to shape a transition, or extend an existing clip. Generate 5–20 seconds at 720p or 1080p with optional audio.
5. **[Luma Ray 3.2](/models/luma-ray-3-2)** — Animate an image or transform existing footage, then explore reframing for another composition. Text and image clips run 5 or 10 seconds.

## Questions before you create
### What is the best image-to-video model?
Start with Seedance 2.5 for a scene that needs time to unfold. Kling Omni Pro and H3 add reference-led options; FLUX 3 gives you explicit first and last frames, while Ray 3.2 is useful when reframing or transforming footage is part of the job.

### Should I use an end frame?
Use one when the landing composition matters. A strong start frame plus a clear end frame usually gives the model a cleaner motion target.

### How do I reduce subject drift?
Keep the action simple, state what must remain unchanged, and use reference-capable models when identity or product geometry matters.
