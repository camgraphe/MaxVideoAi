## What this ranking measures
Product video must protect geometry, materials, labels, and approved brand assets while the camera and story move. A beautiful clip is not useful if the product changes shape.

## Best picks
1. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** - best overall for longer product stories, campaign references, multi-shot reveals, audio, and targeted edits.
2. **[Wan 3 Prime](/models/wan-3-prime)** - best premium Wan route for polished product work across text, frames, and references.
3. **[MiniMax H3 Max](/models/minimax-h3-max)** - best for polished fast production, prompt adherence, clean detail, and native audio.
4. **[LTX 2.5 Pro](/models/ltx-2-5-pro)** - best current LTX production route for text, image, and source-audio-driven product workflows.
5. **[Kling O3 Pro](/models/kling-o3-pro)** - best for controlled product continuity, planned reveals, and recurring props across shots.

## Why these models lead now
Seedance 2.5 handles a full campaign story and a large approved reference pack. Wan 3 Prime and MiniMax H3 Max provide strong current quality and consistency. LTX 2.5 Pro replaces the older 2.3 production recommendation, while Kling O3 Pro remains valuable for explicit product and shot control.

## Product workflow checklist
- Start from the cleanest approved packshot available.
- State which geometry, label, color, and material must not change.
- Use one product action per shot.
- Reserve the last frame for the product or offer.
- Review the full-resolution output before approving packaging or text.

## FAQ
### What is the best model for product videos?
Seedance 2.5 is the strongest general choice. Wan 3 Prime and MiniMax H3 Max are strong current alternatives for polished commercial work.

### Which model is best for fast product variants?
MiniMax H3 Max is a strong fast-production route. Use LTX 2.5 Pro when the workflow needs LTX image or source-audio modes.

### How do I prevent product drift?
Use a clean reference, protect the important geometry in the prompt, and avoid asking the product to perform several unrelated actions in one shot.
