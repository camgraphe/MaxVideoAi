## What this page is for
Product video is stricter than general cinematic video. The product must stay readable, geometry should not drift, surfaces need clean reflections, and the camera should help the buyer understand the item. A beautiful clip is not useful if the product changes shape, the label melts, or the reveal hides the object.

## Best picks
1. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** - best for 30-second product stories, image and campaign references, multi-shot reveals, and end-frame control.
2. **[LTX 2.3 Pro](/models/ltx-2-3-pro)** - best production workflow for extension, retakes, audio-led variants, and 4K-capable finishing.
3. **[Seedance 2.0](/models/seedance-2-0)** - strong alternative for shorter product variants and 4K delivery.
4. **[Kling 3 Pro](/models/kling-3-pro)** - best for structured product reveals, multi-shot demos, Elements, and end-frame planning.
5. **[Happy Horse 1.0](/models/happy-horse-1-0)** - best when product videos need image inputs, R2V references, V2V edits, native audio, or lip-sync in one model.

## Why these models rank here
Seedance 2.5 leads when the product needs a complete cinematic story from approved image or campaign references, with multi-shot continuity and up to 30 seconds. Seedance 2.0 remains the 4K Seedance alternative.

LTX 2.3 Pro is the strongest repair-and-finishing workflow choice. It can extend, retake, and move toward higher-resolution delivery when a product shot is almost right but one section needs repair.

Seedance 2.0 remains useful when a shorter reference-led product variant must be delivered in 4K.

Kling 3 Pro is the best choice for a product story: macro detail, use-case shot, hero reveal, final landing frame. Its multi-shot control and Elements workflow make it easier to keep the product or prop anchored across the sequence.

Happy Horse 1.0 remains useful when product video may move between modes. A team can start from a product image, add R2V references, generate native audio or lip-sync for a social cut, and use V2V editing when the first clip needs a visual change, but Seedance and Kling are stronger for image quality and motion.

## Product prompt checklist
- State what the product is and what must stay stable.
- Keep the product visible in the first or second beat.
- Use simple camera movement: push-in, orbit, rack focus, tabletop slide.
- Avoid asking for readable packaging text unless the source is already clean and controlled.
- Use a final landing frame for ecommerce or ad thumbnails.

## FAQ
### What is the best AI video generator for product videos?
Seedance 2.5 is the best first pick for product videos that need references, longer multi-shot storytelling, and end-frame control. LTX 2.3 Pro is strongest for repair and 4K-capable finishing, while Seedance 2.0 remains a 4K Seedance option.

### Which model is best for ecommerce videos?
Use Seedance 2.5 for reference-led campaign variants, LTX 2.3 Pro for controlled production and finishing, and Kling 3 Pro for structured product reveals. Keep Seedance 2.0 for 4K Seedance delivery.

### How do I avoid product drift?
Use a reference image, keep one product action per shot, and state exactly what must remain unchanged.
