## Your scene comes first
Put the product in a story. Keep its shape recognizable while something interesting happens.

## The shortlist, in detail
1. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** — Use product images alongside visual or motion references to direct a scene up to 30 seconds. Check the shape and details in the result.
2. **[Kling 3.0 Omni Pro](/models/kling-o3-pro)** — A 1080p option for image-led scenes, reference-guided work and video transformation. Plan what each reference should control.
3. **[Wan 3 Prime](/models/wan-3-prime)** — Use opening and ending frames, mix references or extend a clip, with generation lengths up to 30 seconds.
4. **[FLUX 3](/models/flux-3)** — Use a first and last frame to shape a transition, or extend an existing clip. Generate 5–20 seconds at 720p or 1080p with optional audio.
5. **[LTX 2.5 Pro](/models/ltx-2-5-pro)** — Use text or an image, or choose audio-to-video with an owned audio file to guide a 1080p scene.

## Questions before you create
### What is the best model for product videos?
Seedance 2.5 and Kling Omni Pro lead for reference-led product stories. Wan 3 Prime adds source editing, FLUX 3 lets you set the opening and closing image, and LTX 2.5 Pro is useful when a supplied soundtrack drives the scene. Check the product shape in every shot.

### Which model is best for fast product variants?
Keep the product reference and action fixed; vary one creative choice at a time. H3 Max accepts mixed references, while LTX 2.5 Pro offers image and audio-to-video routes. Review the actual quote and turnaround rather than assuming which is fastest.

### How do I prevent product drift?
Use a clean reference, protect the important geometry in the prompt, and avoid asking the product to perform several unrelated actions in one shot.
