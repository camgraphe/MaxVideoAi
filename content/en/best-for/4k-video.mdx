## What this ranking measures
4K is a delivery choice. The winner must preserve detail through motion and offer a credible high-resolution path, not merely attach a 4K label to a weak clip.

## Best picks
1. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** - best current 4K choice for high model quality, multimodal references, multi-shot direction, and native stereo audio.
2. **[Google Veo 3.1](/models/veo-3-1)** - best mature 4K-capable route for polished shots, references, native audio, and extensions.
3. **[Kling 3 4K](/models/kling-3-4k)** - best dedicated Kling route when native 4K output is the main requirement.
4. **[Google Veo 3.1 Fast](/models/veo-3-1-fast)** - best faster Veo route for lower-cost 4K-capable iteration.
5. **[Seedance 2.0](/models/seedance-2-0)** - best Seedance-family option when 4K matters more than the newer 2.5 model's longer 1080p workflow.

## Why MiniMax H3 now leads
MiniMax H3 combines direct 4K output with stronger current benchmark performance, native stereo audio, first/end-frame control, and multimodal references. Veo 3.1 and Kling 3 4K remain proven delivery routes. Seedance 2.0 stays only because it offers a specific 4K path that Seedance 2.5 does not currently replace.

## Use 4K at the right stage
- Draft composition and motion at a cheaper setting first.
- Confirm the model is right for the shot before increasing resolution.
- Check faces, hands, labels, texture, and fine geometry at full size.
- Keep the original output for delivery and use optimized copies for web playback.

## FAQ
### What is the best 4K AI video model?
MiniMax H3 is the strongest current all-around 4K choice on MaxVideoAI. Veo 3.1 remains a reliable polished alternative.

### Does Seedance 2.5 generate 4K?
The current MaxVideoAI Seedance 2.5 route tops out at 1080p. Use Seedance 2.0 when the Seedance look and direct 4K delivery are both required.

### Should every final video be generated in 4K?
No. Generate in 4K only when the destination benefits from it and after the motion, continuity, and composition are already working.
