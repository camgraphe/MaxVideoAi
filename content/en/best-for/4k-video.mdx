## Your scene comes first
A bigger frame is useful when the scene holds up. Compare the 4K options after checking motion and detail.

## The shortlist, in detail
1. **[Kling 3.0 Omni 4K](/models/kling-o3-4k)** — Generate 3–15 second 4K clips from text, an opening image or image references, with optional audio.
2. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** — Generate 5–15 second clips with audio, with output options up to 4K and support for image, video and audio references.
3. **[Google Veo 3.1](/models/veo-3-1)** — Explore text, image and reference workflows, including first-and-last-frame control and 4K output options.
4. **[Gemini Omni Flash 1.1](/models/gemini-omni-flash)** — Create 3–10 second scenes, guide them with images, or edit and extend a supplied clip, with output options up to 4K.
5. **[Seedance 2.0](/models/seedance-2-0)** — Work from text, images or mixed references, transform or extend a clip, with output options up to 4K.

## Questions before you create
### What is the best 4K AI video model?
Kling Omni 4K now leads for 4K delivery with image-reference control. H3 adds mixed references and generated audio; Veo 3.1 adds first-and-last-frame control. Omni Flash 1.1 and Seedance 2.0 cover short revisions and other reference workflows. Check the exact mode and duration.

### Does Seedance 2.5 generate 4K?
The current MaxVideoAI Seedance 2.5 route tops out at 1080p. Use Seedance 2.0 when the Seedance look and direct 4K delivery are both required.

### Should every final video be generated in 4K?
No. Generate in 4K only when the destination benefits from it and after the motion, continuity, and composition are already working.
