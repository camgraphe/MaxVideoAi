## Your scene comes first
Make someone stop scrolling. Start with a clear hook, a person or an action worth watching.

## The shortlist, in detail
1. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** — Build a campaign scene up to 30 seconds around your visual references. Plan the hook, action and closing image before generating.
2. **[Kling 3.0 Omni Pro](/models/kling-o3-pro)** — A 1080p option for image-led scenes, reference-guided work and video transformation. Plan what each reference should control.
3. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** — Generate 5–15 second clips with audio, with output options up to 4K and support for image, video and audio references.
4. **[Wan 3 Prime](/models/wan-3-prime)** — Use opening and ending frames, mix references or extend a clip, with generation lengths up to 30 seconds.
5. **[Gemini Omni Flash 1.1](/models/gemini-omni-flash)** — Create 3–10 second scenes, guide them with images, or edit and extend a supplied clip, with output options up to 4K.

## Questions before you create
### What is the best AI video model for ads?
Start with Seedance 2.5 for a longer campaign scene, or Kling Omni Pro for a storyboard-led spot. H3 covers short scenes with generated sound, Wan 3 Prime adds source-based variants, and Omni Flash 1.1 suits short revisions. Add exact logos and claims during editing.

### Which model is best for product ads?
Use Seedance 2.5 or Wan 3 Prime for complete product stories, MiniMax H3 for high-resolution delivery, and Kling O3 Pro for structured reveals.

### Should ad variants use the same model?
Usually yes. Keep the model and reference pack stable while changing one hook, offer, or ending at a time.
