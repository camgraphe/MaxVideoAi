This MaxVideoAI-owned demonstration uses a generated, unbranded watch image approved for public use. The brief was a restrained product reveal: keep the cobalt strap, metallic case and lime dial details readable, with a fixed camera and a cobalt/lime light sweep behind the watch. Wan 3 Prime suited this setup because it accepted the same image as both first- and last-frame references for the intended return to the starting pose; the motion still needed human review.

**What to inspect.** The blue strap and metallic case remain recognisable while the coloured light moves behind the product. The motion is restrained, so this is a hero reveal rather than a demonstration of the watch's functions. Inspect the small dial markings at full size before using it in an ad. The image has no brand logo: this case does not establish exact logo preservation. Owner acceptance is a creative review of this clip, not a general benchmark win for the model.

## Questions before you create
### What is the best AI video model for ads?
Start with Seedance 2.5 for a longer campaign scene, or Kling Omni Pro for a storyboard-led spot. H3 covers short scenes with generated sound, Wan 3 Prime adds source-based variants, and Omni Flash 1.1 suits short revisions. Add exact logos and claims during editing.

### Which model is best for product ads?
Use Seedance 2.5 or Wan 3 Prime for complete product stories, MiniMax H3 for high-resolution delivery, and Kling O3 Pro for structured reveals.

### Should ad variants use the same model?
Usually yes. Keep the model and reference pack stable while changing one hook, offer, or ending at a time.
