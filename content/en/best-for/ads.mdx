## What this ranking measures
An ad model must keep the product clear, maintain brand references, control the hook and landing frame, and create useful variants without rebuilding the campaign from scratch.

## Best picks
1. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** - best overall for 30-second ads, many campaign references, connected shots, audio, and targeted edits.
2. **[Wan 3 Prime](/models/wan-3-prime)** - best premium Wan route for polished product stories across text, image, and reference workflows.
3. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** - best for high-resolution commercial video with multimodal direction and native stereo audio.
4. **[Kling O3 Pro](/models/kling-o3-pro)** - best for controlled product, character, and storyboard continuity across ad beats.
5. **[Grok Imagine Video 1.5](/models/grok-imagine-video-1-5)** - best for fast image-led social ads with convincing motion, speech, and synchronized sound.

## Why these models lead now
Seedance 2.5 turns a product brief into a longer connected story and can reuse a large campaign reference pack. Wan 3 Prime and MiniMax H3 now offer stronger current quality than older default recommendations. Kling O3 Pro is the control pick, while Grok Imagine Video 1.5 is useful for fast social variants built from approved imagery.

## FAQ
### What is the best AI video model for ads?
Seedance 2.5 is the strongest all-around option for campaign references, longer stories, audio, and variants. Wan 3 Prime is a strong quality-led alternative.

### Which model is best for product ads?
Use Seedance 2.5 or Wan 3 Prime for complete product stories, MiniMax H3 for high-resolution delivery, and Kling O3 Pro for structured reveals.

### Should ad variants use the same model?
Usually yes. Keep the model and reference pack stable while changing one hook, offer, or ending at a time.
