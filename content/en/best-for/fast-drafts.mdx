## What this ranking measures
A draft model should return useful creative information quickly. It must be fast or economical enough for repeated tests while remaining close enough to a production route to validate motion, timing, and composition.

## Best picks
1. **[LTX 2.5 Fast](/models/ltx-2-5-fast)** - best current LTX route for rapid text, image, and source-audio-driven exploration.
2. **[MiniMax H3 Max](/models/minimax-h3-max)** - best for fast polished 768p drafts from text with prompt expansion and native audio.
3. **[Grok Imagine Video 1.5](/models/grok-imagine-video-1-5)** - best for fast image-led motion tests with synchronized sound.
4. **[Seedance 2.0 Fast](/models/seedance-2-0-fast)** - best specialized draft route when the final direction stays in the Seedance family.
5. **[Kling 3 Standard](/models/kling-3-standard)** - best lower-cost Kling route for testing structured shots and Elements.

## Why these models lead now
LTX 2.5 Fast replaces LTX 2.3 Fast as the current speed-oriented LTX model. MiniMax H3 Max combines faster production with much stronger current quality than legacy budget models. Grok Imagine Video 1.5 is valuable when an approved image needs quick motion, while Seedance Fast and Kling Standard remain useful family-specific draft routes.

## Draft efficiently
- Change one creative variable per generation.
- Keep duration short unless timing is the question.
- Test framing and motion before increasing resolution.
- Promote only the strongest draft to the final model.
- Save the prompt and references that produced the useful result.

## FAQ
### What is the best model for fast drafts?
LTX 2.5 Fast is the strongest general draft route. MiniMax H3 Max is better when the draft also needs polished visuals and native audio.

### Should I draft in the same model family as the final?
Use the same family when control behavior must carry over. Otherwise, choose the fastest model that can answer the creative question.

### When is a draft finished?
Stop when composition, motion, timing, and subject behavior are clear enough to choose a production model.
