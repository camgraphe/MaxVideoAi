## Your scene comes first
Try the idea before polishing it. One short scene should answer one creative question.

## The shortlist, in detail
1. **[LTX 2.5 Fast](/models/ltx-2-5-fast)** — Explore text or image-led motion, or use your own audio with audio-to-video. Start with one action you can judge clearly.
2. **[Seedance 2.0 Fast](/models/seedance-2-0-fast)** — Explore text, image and reference ideas at 480p or 720p before committing to a final delivery workflow.
3. **[FLUX 3 Draft](/models/flux-3-draft)** — Test the opening, ending and timing of a 5–20 second scene at 720p, with optional audio and clip extension.
4. **[Google Veo 3.1 Lite](/models/veo-3-1-lite)** — Choose 4, 6 or 8 seconds from text or an image, with 720p or 1080p output and optional audio. First-and-last-frame control is also available.
5. **[Kling 3.0 Turbo Standard](/models/kling-3-turbo-standard)** — Explore a 3–15 second scene from text or an image at 720p, with sound generated alongside the picture.

## Questions before you create
### What is the best model for fast drafts?
Start with LTX 2.5 Fast to test movement or audio-led timing. Seedance 2.0 Fast adds mixed references; FLUX 3 Draft gives explicit start and end frames. Veo 3.1 Lite and Kling Turbo Standard round out short-scene options. This order reflects iteration controls, not measured speed or a cheapest-price ranking.

### Should I draft in the same model family as the final?
Keep the same model and settings when you need a direct comparison. Changing variants can change controls and the result; a draft is not a preview of a guaranteed final render.

### When is a draft finished?
Stop when composition, motion, timing, and subject behavior are clear enough to choose a production model.
