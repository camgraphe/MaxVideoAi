## What this ranking measures
A multi-shot model must do more than insert random cuts. It should organize a setup, development, transition, and landing beat while preserving subjects, locations, voices, and visual language.

## Best picks
1. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** - best overall for coherent 30-second stories, references, audio, extensions, and targeted edits.
2. **[Wan 3 Prime](/models/wan-3-prime)** - best premium Wan route for connected text, image, and reference-led sequences.
3. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** - best for native multi-shot video with multimodal references and stereo audio up to 4K.
4. **[Kling O3 Pro](/models/kling-o3-pro)** - best for deliberately structured sequences with reusable subjects and strong continuity.
5. **[Wan 3](/models/wan-3)** - best standard Wan route for strong sequence quality without choosing Prime.

## Why these models lead now
Seedance 2.5 owns the broadest long-form workflow. Wan 3 and Wan 3 Prime score strongly for sequencing and current blind preference. MiniMax H3 natively models multiple shots with audio, while Kling O3 Pro remains the strongest control-oriented option for planned scene beats.

## Prompt structure
1. Define the stable subject, setting, style, and audio language.
2. Give each shot one framing, action, and camera move.
3. Name the transition or continuity requirement between shots.
4. Reserve the final beat for the reveal, payoff, or landing frame.
5. Keep dialogue short enough to match the planned timing.

## FAQ
### What is the best model for multi-shot video?
Seedance 2.5 is the strongest general option for a complete 30-second sequence. Wan 3 Prime and MiniMax H3 are strong current alternatives.

### Is multi-shot the same as video editing?
No. Multi-shot generation creates several connected beats. Editing changes or assembles footage after generation, although Seedance 2.5 can also handle targeted edits.

### Which model gives the most explicit control?
Kling O3 Pro is a strong choice when planned subjects, props, and shot structure matter more than maximum duration.
