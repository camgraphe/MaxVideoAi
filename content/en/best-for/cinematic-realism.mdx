## Your scene comes first
A look, a movement, a believable moment. Choose the model around the scene you want to direct.

## The shortlist, in detail
1. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** — Build a scene up to 30 seconds, starting with text, an image or a mix of visual and audio references.
2. **[Wan 3 Prime](/models/wan-3-prime)** — Use opening and ending frames, mix references or extend a clip, with generation lengths up to 30 seconds.
3. **[Google Veo 3.1](/models/veo-3-1)** — Explore text, image and reference workflows, including first-and-last-frame control and 4K output options.
4. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** — Generate 5–15 second clips with audio, with output options up to 4K and support for image, video and audio references.
5. **[Luma Ray 3.2](/models/luma-ray-3-2)** — Animate an image or transform existing footage, then explore reframing for another composition. Text and image clips run 5 or 10 seconds.

## Questions before you create
### What is the best cinematic AI video model?
Seedance 2.5 leads this selection for longer, reference-led scenes. Wan 3 Prime adds source editing and continuation. Veo 3.1 and H3 offer 4K options; Ray 3.2 suits a visual workflow where you will add the sound separately.

### Is 4K necessary for cinematic work?
No. Direction, motion, and lighting matter first. Use MiniMax H3 or another 4K-capable route after the creative direction is approved.

### Which model is best for structured scenes?
Compare Kling O3 Pro’s reference workflow with Seedance 2.5 and Wan 3 Prime using the same shot plan. Reference support is useful, but exact cuts and subject continuity still require review.
