## What this page is for
Reference-to-video is broader than animating one image. A project can combine approved products, characters, motion, camera language, style, source video, or audio. The best model understands which reference controls which part of the result.

## Best picks
1. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** - best overall for large multimodal reference packs, 30-second sequences, editing, and native audio.
2. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** - best for unified visual, motion, video, and voice references with native stereo sound.
3. **[Wan 3 Prime](/models/wan-3-prime)** - best premium Wan route for image, video, audio, and first/last-frame guidance.
4. **[Kling O3 Pro](/models/kling-o3-pro)** - best for controlled reference-led scenes and reusable subjects or props.
5. **[Grok Imagine Video 1.5](/models/grok-imagine-video-1-5)** - best for short clips guided by up to several image references and optional voice direction.

## Why these models lead now
The current frontier treats references as a production system rather than a single uploaded image. Seedance 2.5 offers the broadest long-form control. MiniMax H3 combines modalities in one model. Wan 3 Prime covers flexible production inputs, while Kling O3 Pro and Grok Imagine Video 1.5 provide focused control for recurring subjects and short clips.

## Reference workflow checklist
- Give every reference a named role.
- State what must remain unchanged and what may be reinterpreted.
- Use motion references only for the movement you want to transfer.
- Keep product, character, and style references visually compatible.
- Test a short version before spending on the final duration.

## FAQ
### What is the best reference-to-video model?
Seedance 2.5 is the most complete current choice. MiniMax H3 is especially strong when visual and audio references must work together.

### Is reference-to-video the same as image-to-video?
No. Image-to-video usually anchors the first frame. Reference-to-video can guide identity, style, motion, voice, or camera language without forcing every reference into the opening frame.

### Which model is best for product references?
Start with Seedance 2.5, Wan 3 Prime, or Kling O3 Pro. Use a clean packshot and explicitly protect geometry, materials, and branding.
