## Your scene comes first
A face, a place, a gesture, a sound. Give each reference one clear job.

## The shortlist, in detail
1. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** — Build a scene up to 30 seconds, starting with text, an image or a mix of visual and audio references.
2. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** — Generate 5–15 second clips with audio, with output options up to 4K and support for image, video and audio references.
3. **[Kling 3.0 Omni Pro](/models/kling-o3-pro)** — A 1080p option for image-led scenes, reference-guided work and video transformation. Plan what each reference should control.
4. **[Wan 3 Prime](/models/wan-3-prime)** — Use opening and ending frames, mix references or extend a clip, with generation lengths up to 30 seconds.
5. **[Gemini Omni Flash 1.1](/models/gemini-omni-flash)** — Create 3–10 second scenes, guide them with images, or edit and extend a supplied clip, with output options up to 4K.

## Questions before you create
### What is the best reference-to-video model?
Seedance 2.5 starts the list for mixed references and longer scenes. H3 adds a 4K option, Kling Omni Pro suits storyboard direction, and Wan 3 Prime can draw on a broader brief. Omni Flash 1.1 is an alternative for image references and short source edits.

### Is reference-to-video the same as image-to-video?
No. Image-to-video usually anchors the first frame. Reference-to-video can guide identity, style, motion, voice, or camera language without forcing every reference into the opening frame.

### Which model is best for product references?
Start with Seedance 2.5, Wan 3 Prime, or Kling O3 Pro. Use a clean packshot and explicitly protect geometry, materials, and branding.
