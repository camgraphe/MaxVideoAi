## What this ranking measures
Stylized video needs stable line work, palette, shading, silhouettes, and character identity. The best model should preserve a visual system through motion instead of drifting toward generic realism.

## Best picks
1. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** - best overall for longer stylized sequences, large reference packs, motion guidance, audio, and editing.
2. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** - best for unified style, subject, motion, and audio references with strong current quality.
3. **[Kling O3 Pro](/models/kling-o3-pro)** - best for controlled stylized characters, props, and planned scene beats.
4. **[Grok Imagine Video 1.5](/models/grok-imagine-video-1-5)** - best for fast stylized animation from approved character or illustration references.
5. **[LTX 2.5 Fast](/models/ltx-2-5-fast)** - best for rapid style and motion exploration before a production pass.

## Why these models replace the old defaults
The current models offer much stronger consistency, references, motion, and audio than legacy loop-focused engines. Seedance 2.5 and MiniMax H3 can hold a broader visual language. Kling O3 Pro provides deliberate continuity, while Grok Imagine Video 1.5 and LTX 2.5 Fast cover quick image-led and exploratory work.

## Stylized prompting checklist
- Define line weight, palette, shading, and background detail.
- Use reference images from the same visual system.
- Keep motion readable and avoid stacking many actions.
- Repeat the same character anchors across every beat.
- Test a short motion pass before requesting a longer sequence.

## FAQ
### What is the best model for anime-style video?
Seedance 2.5 is the strongest broad choice for longer referenced sequences. MiniMax H3 and Kling O3 Pro are strong alternatives for multimodal or controlled character work.

### Which model is best for a quick stylized loop?
Use Grok Imagine Video 1.5 for an image-led clip or LTX 2.5 Fast for rapid style and motion tests.

### How do I stop the style from drifting?
Use compatible references, define a compact visual system, and avoid mixing several unrelated art directions in one prompt.
