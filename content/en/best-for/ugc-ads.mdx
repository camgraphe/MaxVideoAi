## What this ranking measures
Good UGC should feel direct and believable: stable faces, natural timing, short dialogue, synchronized audio, simple handheld framing, and enough control to produce repeatable variants.

## Best picks
1. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** - best overall for longer creator stories, references, native audio, dialogue, and repeatable variants.
2. **[Happy Horse 1.1](/models/happy-horse-1-1)** - best for strong lip sync and audio-led creator clips in a unified workflow.
3. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** - best for high-quality speaking characters with multimodal references and native stereo sound.
4. **[Grok Imagine Video 1.5](/models/grok-imagine-video-1-5)** - best for fast image-led creator clips with improved motion, speech, and audio sync.
5. **[Kling O3 Pro](/models/kling-o3-pro)** - best when the UGC concept needs recurring characters, products, or several planned beats.

## Why these models lead now
Seedance 2.5 combines the strongest current audio, motion, consistency, and sequencing scores. Happy Horse 1.1 replaces the older 1.0 recommendation for lip-sync-led workflows. MiniMax H3 and Grok Imagine Video 1.5 bring stronger current audio-video generation, while Kling O3 Pro handles structured creator sequences.

## UGC prompting checklist
- Keep each spoken line short.
- Use simple camera movement during dialogue.
- Ask for natural pauses, blinking, breathing, and small head motion.
- Protect the product and face with clear references.
- Change one hook or call to action per variant.

## FAQ
### What is the best AI video model for UGC?
Seedance 2.5 is the strongest overall choice. Happy Horse 1.1 is especially useful when lip sync is the main requirement.

### Which model is best for a talking-head ad?
Start with Seedance 2.5, Happy Horse 1.1, or MiniMax H3. Keep the line short and the camera calm.

### Is polished video always better for UGC?
No. Believable timing and performance matter more than a glossy commercial look.
