## Your scene comes first
A believable voice and a moment that feels lived. Build the scene around the performance.

## The shortlist, in detail
1. **[MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3)** — Create a 5–15 second character scene with generated sound. Image, video and audio references can guide the person, action and spoken moment.
2. **[Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5)** — Build a scene up to 30 seconds, starting with text, an image or a mix of visual and audio references.
3. **[Happy Horse 1.1](/models/happy-horse-1-1)** — Create from text, an image or references with audio generated alongside the video, up to 1080p.
4. **[Grok Imagine Video 1.5](/models/grok-imagine-video-1-5)** — Animate an opening image with generated audio, or explore a scene from text. Both routes offer output up to 1080p.
5. **[Google Veo 3.1](/models/veo-3-1)** — Explore text, image and reference workflows, including first-and-last-frame control and 4K output options.

## Questions before you create
### What is the best AI video model for UGC?
H3 moves first for short character scenes with generated sound and mixed references. Seedance 2.5 gives a longer scene room to breathe. HappyHorse 1.1, Grok 1.5 and Veo 3.1 are alternatives to test with the same spoken line and framing.

### Which model is best for a talking-head ad?
Start with Seedance 2.5, Happy Horse 1.1, or MiniMax H3. Keep the line short and the camera calm.

### Is polished video always better for UGC?
No. Believable timing and performance matter more than a glossy commercial look.
