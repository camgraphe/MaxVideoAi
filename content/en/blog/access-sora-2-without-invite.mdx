Updated September 21, 2026. This article originally described access to Sora 2 through MaxVideoAI. That access is now closed for new generations.

OpenAI announces the shutdown of the Sora API on **September 24, 2026**. The [official discontinuation notice](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001152-what-to-know-about-the-sora-discontinuation) explains the schedule. [fal.ai also announces the endpoint shutdown](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/sora-2/text-to-video). MaxVideoAI has closed new Sora jobs ahead of that deadline.

## Choose a model for a new video

- [Seedance 2.5](/models/seedance-2-5): the first alternative to evaluate for your next project.
- [MiniMax H3](/models/minimax-h3): another current model to compare against your prompt and reference needs.
- [Wan 3](/models/wan-3): a third current option to evaluate for your workflow.

These models have their own controls, supported inputs and prices. Check the model page and the quote before generating; Sora settings and output quality are not guaranteed to transfer unchanged. MaxVideoAI does not silently submit an old Sora project to another model.

## Keep your existing work

Your existing MaxVideoAI videos remain in [your library](/app/library). Closing new generation does not delete those videos or their job history. You can keep an old prompt and explicitly choose a current model for a new job.

The [Sora 2 archive](/models/sora-2), [Sora 2 Pro archive](/models/sora-2-pro) and [historical examples](/examples/sora) remain available. They document past results and do not offer new Sora generations.
