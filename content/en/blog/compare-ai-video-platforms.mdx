## Check the last step before you pay

Run a small brief all the way to the result you need. For a clip, check that the model version, mode and settings are available with the plan you intend to use. For a finished film, check what you can revise and export, including the audio and the rights that apply. For an assistant, confirm which host can run the workflow and which account pays for the media.

A fair price comparison keeps the model version, mode, duration, resolution, aspect ratio, audio, references and number of completed attempts aligned. Record the plan, billing period, currency and checkout conditions alongside the price you see. Platform terms above were checked against public documentation on 10 November 2026.
