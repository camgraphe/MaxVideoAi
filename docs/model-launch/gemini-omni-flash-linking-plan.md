# Gemini Omni Flash Internal Linking Plan

Research Date: 2026-09-03

## Anchor Rules

- Use concise, descriptive anchors with “1.1” in visible product labels while keeping the canonical slug unchanged.
- Link to canonical owners, never version aliases.
- Keep comparison pages balanced between both model pages.
- Avoid repeated exact-match anchors inside the same section.

## Links To Avoid

- Do not link to `/models/gemini-omni-flash-1-1`.
- Do not turn every Google or Veo mention into an Omni link.
- Do not expose internal engineering or provider-routing URLs.
- Avoid generic, non-descriptive anchor text.

## Placement Limits

| Surface | Limit |
| --- | --- |
| model page | workspace, family examples, pricing, specs and 1–2 comparisons |
| comparison page | two primary model links plus contextual related comparisons |
| pricing row | one model-detail link and one workspace CTA |

## Verification Notes

- Confirm crawlable localized anchors and reciprocal hreflang.
- Confirm aliases redirect once to the existing canonical.
- Confirm no new canonical is generated for the 1.1 alias.
