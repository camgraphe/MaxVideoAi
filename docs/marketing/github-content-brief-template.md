## Evidence and language check

- Use current product proof before decorative art. A decorative illustration never proves host compatibility, prices, approval, benchmarks, or a result.
- Qualify host, model, privacy, and price claims beside the sentence they change.
- Do not invent statistics, testimonials, rankings, completed host runs, or a directory/listing outcome.
- Do not buy links, automate unsolicited outreach, or request private media or personal contact data.
- If a publication gate is closed, keep this as a draft or publish only the evidence-qualified part that is independently useful.
