**Audience:** English-speaking people who already use Claude Desktop to develop a project or product brief and need an individual short clip. **Job:** bring that brief to a compatible MaxVideoAI connection, review available models and an exact quote, explicitly approve generation, then recover the clip. **Destination:** `/integrations/claude`; `/mcp` and pricing remain support resources. **CTA:** set up MaxVideoAI in Claude. **Proof:** existing public sample video plus dated controlled Claude result, clearly distinguished from illustrated connection/quote stages. A fresh complete host recording is still required before calling the pack a current end-to-end demonstration.

Codex and Studio are prepared alternatives; do not split this first budget among them. The production sequence-export flag remains disabled and no assembled MP4 is promised.

No automatic expansion or replenishment. Keep production/generation capture costs outside this media budget and obtain distinct authorization. Use an available enforceable total/lifetime limit and end date; if the platform cannot bound the cost within this ceiling, do not launch with that setup.

## Measurement and decisions

Primary learning question: do qualified visitors complete the connection and return to the assistant? Record visits, setup action, accounts created, effective authorized MCP connections, completed media and confirmed first external payments independently. A copied address or install CTA is not an effective connection. Payment can precede or follow generation; do not force an ordered funnel. Count first live external payers separately from repeat credit purchases. The candidate receipt-history flag does not yet establish that all-flow first-payer contract; direct-card capture remains a prerequisite before reporting it. Keep GA4 consented reporting separate from the payment ledger and provider cost/margin. The legacy Ads success-return signal is not verified payment evidence and cannot support purchase/CPA optimization.
