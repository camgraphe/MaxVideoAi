# ByteDance family release review

Date: 2026-09-29. Branch: `codex/bytedance-pricing-grid`. This is the evidence and decision brief for [the marketing follow-through plan](../superpowers/plans/2026-09-28-bytedance-marketing-follow-through.md); it is not a publication or customer-price change.

## Decisions from current evidence

1. Prepare Seedance 1.5 Pro (`seedance-1-5-pro`) for a localized `deep_legacy` archive. Keep the three model URLs and historical comparisons/examples. Close new generation and discoverability through the authored registry, while retaining saved jobs, provider-specific polling, receipts, refunds and original media. Do not map an existing job or paid request to another model.
2. Do not 301 the 1.5 model page to Fast or Mini now. Search Console shows distinct 1.5 and 1.5-versus-2.0 intent, and neither Fast nor Mini is a complete 1.5 capability match. The archive should explain three separate onward paths: Mini for a lower-cost **Seedance 2.x** option, Fast for speed/cost balance, and 2.5 for currently supported 1080p. Mention 2.5 Draft only after its customer workflow and price are released.
3. Do not market Mini as MaxVideoAI's cheapest video model at current prices. The exact 5-second 720p public matrix already contains cheaper active alternatives. Compare effective production quotes and approve any new tariff separately before claiming a new entry price.
4. Keep the 1.5 direct BytePlus bridge disabled. Console activation was followed by `InvalidEndpointOrModel.NotFound` in the 2026-09-29 canary. The [direct bridge record](../engineering/byteplus-seedance-1-5-direct.md) and shutdown guard remain historical/operational evidence. No BytePlus support contact is authorized.

## Release sequence and acceptance

1. Close new 1.5 generation and publication with focused red/green regression tests; prove historical jobs, receipts, refunds and media survive. Ship the three localized archives as the same lifecycle change. Verify before BytePlus's 2026-11-11 shutdown.
2. Run a separate factual-content pass for Fast/Mini, Seedream Pro resolution and the live Seedance family. Keep 2.5 Draft copy behind its customer release gate. Keep current customer prices unchanged.
3. Verify exact local and deployed HTTP/HTML, canonical/hreflang, structured data, sitemap, robots/WAF, watch video and first Play. Record Search Console indexing, impressions, clicks, query mix and any AI-search referrals before and after rollout; no ranking or citation outcome is guaranteed.
