This MaxVideoAI workflow offers 480p or 720p output, 4–30 second generation and optional audio, with separate Create, Modify and Extend actions.How do I create a Seedance 2.5 Flash video?Open Create with Flash and describe the scene, action, camera and sound. Add a first frame, an optional end frame or supported references for the workflow you choose. Select the output settings, review the price and generate. Use Modify or Extend when you want to work from an existing video.What image, video and audio references can I use?Flash accepts up to 10 images, 5 videos and 5 audio clips, subject to the selected mode. Images can guide the subject or setting; videos can guide movement and framing; audio can guide the sound. Reference videos share a 30-second total limit, and audio references require a visual input.Can Seedance 2.5 Flash generate sound?Yes. Enable generated audio and describe dialogue, ambience or sound effects alongside the action. Audio references guide the request; they are different from enabling audio output. Review timing and intelligibility in the generated result.What is the difference between Modify and Extend?Modify asks for a change inside a source video and follows its aspect ratio and approximate duration. Extend asks for a continuation before or after a source clip. Choose the source excerpt and describe the change or continuation explicitly; check movement, identity and sound in the result.How is Flash different from standard Seedance 2.5?In MaxVideoAI, Flash offers 480p or 720p. Standard Seedance 2.5 also offers 1080p and a native Draft-to-final workflow with different reference limits. A normal Flash generation is not a finalizable Draft. Compare the settings and current quotes for the version you need.How much does Seedance 2.5 Flash cost?The price depends on the selected duration, resolution, audio and source inputs. Reference videos can affect billable duration.
