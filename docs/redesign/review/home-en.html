How do I create an AI video?+

Describe your scene or upload a reference image. Choose a compatible model, set the duration and format, check the price and start generating.

Which AI video model should I choose?+

It depends on the shot: motion, realism, audio, references and budget. Watch real results and compare the models’ capabilities for your use case.

Can I start from an image?+

Yes, with a model that supports image to video. Your image is the starting point for the composition; describe the motion you want.

Text to video or image to video?+

Text to video starts from a written description. Image to video starts from an existing visual. Choose according to the control you need over the subject and composition.

Where can I find examples and prompts?+

The gallery includes videos, their models and available generation details. Comparisons and use-case guides help you prepare your next shot.

How much does a generation cost?+

The price depends on the model, duration, resolution and options. The cost is shown before generation. Visit pricing to compare configurations.

Do I need a subscription?+

No. MaxVideoAI uses pay-as-you-go credits. Top up when needed; failed generations are automatically credited back.

Which limits should I check first?+

Check supported modes, duration, resolution, aspect ratio, audio and accepted references. Capabilities vary by model and settings.
