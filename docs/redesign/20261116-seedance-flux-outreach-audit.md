# Seedance 2.5 and FLUX 3 Max: Outreach evidence for the cinematic migration

Read-only audit, 16 November 2026. Scope: existing model URLs, their family pages, useful copy and citation opportunities. No production changes, outreach messages or publishing actions were performed.

## Decision

Proceed with an incremental migration of the existing URLs, preserving their metadata, localized equivalents, original examples, useful answers and internal links. Give Seedance 2.5 its native Draft → final workflow and FLUX 3 Max its timed keyframe workflow. These are product differentiators; the available GSC data does **not** establish demand for either feature as a search query.

Keep the model pages focused on using that exact model. Family galleries should own browsing examples across versions, and comparison pages should own the detailed “versus” decision. Shared family terminology is not, by itself, evidence of harmful cannibalization.

## Copy and links to retain or improve

### Seedance 2.5 standard

- Preserve the existing SEO title, **“Seedance 2.5 AI Video Generator: 30s, 1080p & Audio”**, description, canonical and localized identity. Put the same factual definition in readable body text: 4–30 seconds, up to 1080p, optional audio, text/images/references and supported video workflows.
- Lead the differentiated section with a real question or benefit: **“Preview in Draft. Finish the take you choose.”** Explain native Draft → final as a product workflow, including separately quoted generations and inherited settings. Do not imply every normal 480p render is a native Draft.
- Retain first/last-frame guidance, multimodal references, Modify/Extend and the practical continuity checks. The cinematic layout must not silently discard these controls just because Draft is visually stronger.
- Keep `/examples/seedance`, `#prompting`, `/pricing#seedance-2-5-pricing` and `/ai-video-engines/seedance-2-0-vs-seedance-2-5?order=seedance-2-5`. Add the direct standard-versus-Flash comparison already supported by the catalog: `/ai-video-engines/seedance-2-5-vs-seedance-2-5-flash`.
- Use the standard page to explain its own final-resolution and Draft workflow. Use a short sibling choice with a comparison link instead of duplicating Flash’s entire sales argument or example descriptions.
- Preserve the useful concept → story beats → continuity method. Frame timing as story-dependent, not equal-length shots or a guaranteed edit list.
- Correct old proof metadata when the selected media changes. The old FAQ says “24-second hero”; the new 28-second FPV hero cannot retain that assertion. Verify reference limits against the current integration rather than copying stale “one to three clips” prose.

### FLUX 3 Max

- Preserve **“FLUX 3 Max | AI Video, Audio & Frame Control | MaxVideoAI”** and its description. Keep the short video-model definition accessible near the hero, then the implemented 5–20-second duration, optional audio, 720p and 1080p upscaling details.
- Make timed keyframes a tangible demonstration: the three actual images, their positions, the resulting film and the reuse recipe. Explain that keyframes guide visual states rather than guaranteeing cuts or serving as unrestricted character references.
- Keep the four original film recipes accessible: **Still Here**, **After the Bell**, **Someone Answers**, **The Door to Spring**. A single selected prompt plus a gallery can work if the remaining prompts, settings and reuse actions remain reachable on their respective examples.
- Retain `/examples/flux`, `/models/flux-3`, `/ai-video-engines/flux-3-vs-flux-3-max?order=flux-3-max`, `/pricing#flux-3-max-pricing`, and the direct Create action with `engine=flux-3-max`.
- Preserve the concrete distinction that standard FLUX 3 has an extension workflow and Max currently does not. Do not transplant Seedance’s Create/Modify/Extend navigation into unsupported Max modes.
- Keep the access-without-your-own-fal-key answer, duration/resolution answer and pricing answer. Any fixed price needs a date or a live pricing source; the current quote remains authoritative before generation.
- Adapt legacy FAQ promises to the rendered template: “Prompt Lab shows four films,” “this page shows current scenario prices,” and references to an on-page editorial score are only valid if the corresponding content remains visible or clearly linked. Use genuine questions for any added FAQ; merge the new keyframe answer with the existing one.
- Existing provider latency, Artificial Analysis and editorial-score claims must stay explicitly attributed and dated if retained. They are not MaxVideoAI measured delivery performance or a controlled model benchmark.

## Citation opportunities and limits

### Observable Bing evidence

The latest available read-only review selects **11 October–9 November**, with the available daily series ending on 8 November. The native UI table was transcribed across all three pages: 68 URLs, 797 citations; its summary shows 815, a difference that remains unresolved. These are citations, not visits or conversions.

- The French and Spanish FLUX 3-versus-Max comparisons each have **1**.
- `/examples/seedance` has **5**.
- The exact English target model URLs are absent from this particular table. Absence is not evidence that they have never been cited.
- Comparisons account for 438 and example galleries for 141 of the 797 page-row citations. This is a site-level observation, not a prediction for the two migrated pages.

Source: `OUTREACH/sources/2026-11-10/llm-citations-competitive-review/bing-30d-pages-ui-derived.json`, `README.md` and `provenance.json`. The five available grounding-query rows do not provide a page × query join.

### Useful, citable content to publish within the existing pages

1. **A named implementation table.** Caption it “Seedance 2.5 settings in MaxVideoAI” or “FLUX 3 Max settings in MaxVideoAI,” with a verification date and a stable `#specs` link. Keep an HTML table, concise row labels, units, native-versus-upscaled distinctions and relevant limitations. Copy/CSV should preserve the model name, date and source URL, rather than exporting an unattributed list. Do not force backlinks.
2. **A reproducible original example.** Connect the actual film to its exact prompt, references, settings and workflow. For keyframes, the image positions are unusually useful evidence. For Draft, distinguish a real linked Draft/final pair from an explanatory workflow illustration.
3. **A compact choice explanation.** Link the existing comparison and sibling model pages where a user genuinely needs a different capability. Avoid an extra thin “best model” page or duplicated comparison paragraphs.
4. **Honest price context.** Label a price as current quote/scenario or historical generation cost as appropriate. Do not let a historical media receipt masquerade as the current price after a tariff change.

These make the pages easier to verify and reuse; backlinks or AI citations are not guaranteed.

### Authoritative source placement

- Link the [fal keyframe API reference](https://fal.ai/models/blackforestlabs/flux-3-max/keyframes-to-video/api) beside the keyframe constraints. It documents 1–10 images, unique frame positions and the 24-fps mapping. **Scope warning:** its current resolution enum also includes 2K and 4K upscaling, while this migration’s MaxVideoAI integration exposes 720p/1080p. Do not advertise provider-only choices as app capabilities.
- Link the [fal prompting guide](https://fal.ai/learn/tools/flux-3-max-prompting-guide) in the practical prompt guidance. Its structure supports clear camera direction, ordered action and an explicit sound instruction. Reference imagery should anchor appearance while text directs movement.
- The [BytePlus Seedance tutorial](https://docs.byteplus.com/en/docs/ModelArk/seedance-2-5) was reachable but its detailed body did not load in this audit. Do not present that fetch as independent verification of every Draft or workflow limit. Use verified integration contracts for the “in MaxVideoAI” table, and validate any more precise provider citation before attaching it to a claim.
- [Google’s AI search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) calls for the usual indexable, useful content and readable text, supported by relevant media and consistent structured data. There is no special AI markup or file requirement that justifies adding filler or promising GEO gains.

## Release checks and measurement

- Compare existing versus new HTML for exact title/description, canonical, hreflang, H1, meaningful section anchors, schema-visible-content consistency, localized routes and sitemap assumptions.
- Verify every retained internal path, engine/workflow parameter, prompt reuse, reference recipe and gallery/version selection; a family gallery must not label a sibling’s output as this exact model.
- Check that prompts, table facts and important answers are available as text; the visual redesign should not turn them into screenshot-only information.
- Preserve poster-first media and lazy manual playback. Check comparable mobile layout and loading evidence; a prettier page with a slower first meaningful image is not automatically a gain.
- Keep this baseline. Review comparable 7-day and 28-day windows after release, plus Create/reuse actions where measured. For Seedance, acquire an exact page-filtered query export before claiming a query-level change or cannibalization. Separate changing demand from page CTR/position and avoid causal conclusions from one week.
