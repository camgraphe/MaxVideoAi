# Milo credit recovery

Approved in the current product review: all chat requires sign-in, including Lite and Studio. Lite remains free under existing availability/fair-use rules. Standard and Max share the existing monthly and purchased credits; no new allowance, tariff or automatic purchase.

The selected approach is an explicit one-click transition. A declined message stays editable. A compact application-authored notice offers **Send with Lite** and **Add credits**. The first selects Lite and sends the current draft once; the second opens the existing credits panel without sending. Keep Lite selected until the customer chooses another level. Automatic model changes and a permanently locked tier selector were considered and rejected because they obscure the customer's choice.

Authentication, insufficient credits, unavailable sponsored service, pending/unknown usage, request-size limits and incomplete output are different states. Only a known pre-dispatch credit rejection permits a new send. Unknown or already-dispatched calls retain existing recovery identities. Keep manual prompt access available. Never claim Lite can perform a new video/audio analysis.

Studio uses the same notice and choices. Newly accepted Studio tasks pause for funding instead of silently switching models. A paused task can explicitly continue with Lite from its saved work, without replaying completed operations or resetting credits/call limits. Historical task snapshots retain their original recovery behavior. No changes to media generation confirmations.

Visual scope: lightweight inline notice, existing panel, English composer copy and existing Studio localization. No player/rail redesign, guest chat, automatic conversation compaction, production migration or deployment.
