## Product contract

The conversation remains the entry point. Its catalog and model details describe
the current executable, certified operations, parameter domains and reference
roles. Studio adapts those facts to its session and project; it does not own a
second price engine, model registry or provider dispatcher.

Visual preparation supports the canonical video modes that can be represented by
owned media and public settings. Private provider interaction identifiers remain
private. An exact model/block/workflow certification is required before discovery.
Audio preparation exposes the canonical available audio packs through one
general preparation tool; the existing voice and music tools remain replayable.
Seed voice cloning uses an explicitly selected owned audio reference.

References use typed canonical identities, never model-supplied storage URLs.
Selected attachments and ready owned project outputs are checked for kind,
ownership and availability before any draft, output promotion or quote mutation.
Canonical preparation and confirmation revalidate media facts and settings. The
client sees a persisted exact quote and confirms it through the existing action.
The assistant cannot purchase media or change a previously accepted quote.
