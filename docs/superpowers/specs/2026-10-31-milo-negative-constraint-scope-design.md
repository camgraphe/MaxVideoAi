# Milo negative-constraint scope

Date: 2026-10-31. Authorized scope: clarify how composer Milo retains negative constraints when preparing creative text.

## Intent and approach

A narrow creative restriction can become a broader ban in Milo's proposed text. Preserve each restriction's intended subject and extent, including the difference between an element being unnecessary and forbidden. Keep explicit broad exclusions and supported setting constraints, while allowing reasonable choices in unspecified creative details.

Clarify the shared provider instructions, the existing owner of semantic writing policy. The decoder must continue to preserve valid proposed text; keyword removal or a second translation/rewrite call would lose legitimate exclusions and introduce another owner. Model selection, guide capabilities and UI retain their existing responsibilities.

## Boundaries and evidence

- No schema, destination protocol, parser, application proof, history, storage, billing, setting projection or model-selection change.
- No negative-language detector, phrase blacklist or output rewriting.
- Preserve language rules, requested literals, audio constraints, creative detail and supported settings.
- A requirement to communicate without a slogan does not imply a ban on every other visible word or mark.
- Persisted request input, source reconstruction and the persisted/displayed proposal locate the observed issue at semantic proposal formulation. They are not a byte-exact capture of provider network input; the model's internal cause is not observed.
- Offline tests verify policy inclusion, unchanged input boundaries and lossless decoding of narrow and broad negatives. They do not establish real-model semantic compliance.

## Verification

Cover narrow headline restrictions with a requested label, an explicitly blank label with other lettering, an explicit complete text ban, a follow-up correcting an earlier broad proposal, and silent video retaining a requested visible sign. Use existing focused contracts, TypeScript, lint, exposure and a source build when feasible. Independent review and live semantic evaluation remain separately owned release gates.
