# Milo launch experience

Approved direction: human language, visual explanation and discoverability for MaxVideoAI’s creative AI. Current PR #555 owns the work.

## Experience
One public EN/FR/ES landing at `/ai-video-prompt-generator`: a clear promise, illustrated editable example showing idea → conversation → reviewed prompt, three concise contextual controls (model, references, settings), honest free/credit levels, and separate paths for the generator, Studio and MCP. The illustration must be labelled as an example, never passed off as an observed Milo response or measured savings. Avoid a gratuitous generated image; existing owned visual assets and interface illustrations explain the product better. Paid generation cap $10, spend starts at $0.

CTA opens the assistant tab in the video generator, retaining account requirements and never sending a message or generating media automatically. A discreet help link explains Milo from the composer. Homepage, Studio, pricing and Getting Started offer contextual discovery. No foundational model names in public plan descriptions.

## Constraints
Preserve player, gallery, credit accounting and provider restrictions. Merge current main into the feature branch. No production merge, migrations or activation in this increment. Public marketing must ship together with enabled product. No promised financial savings, unlimited free messages, full automatic reference analysis, or made-up customer proof. Existing consent analytics only; no raw prompts or references in analytics.

## Release
Focused interaction/localization/SEO tests, TypeScript, lint, build, mobile/desktop visual check and required CI on current main. Prepare explicit release/rollback checklist and list outstanding retention/customer-information decisions.
