# English video background changer landing

`app/tools/video-background-changer/page.tsx` is an English use-case route. Its server view contains the approved explanation and links to the existing Wan 3 Prime `v2v` workspace. It is not a new Creator Tool, upload endpoint or generation workflow.

The route returns `notFound()` while `_lib/media.ts` has no qualified public pair. Do not activate it with private/signed URLs or local review files. Public activation requires separately approved copies of the exact complete source/result and posters, byte/HTTP/Range checks, authored video identities in `config/public-video-sources.json`, and one English-only sitemap entry. Keep canonical/hreflang English only. The shared language switch and `lang` middleware use `marketing-page-fallback.ts` to send French/Spanish choices to their existing tools hubs; absent translations stay 404.

`useVideoBackgroundComparison` owns pair timing, pending seek, source/result/off sound, buffering and the full end of both files. Both attempts delegate original selection, errors/retry and measurement to `usePublicVideoControls`. Its optional visibility target lets the whole pair remain playable when stacked mobile tracks are individually outside the viewport. Existing callers retain node-level visibility. Additive `pause` and `setMuted` methods let the pair coordinate deterministically without duplicating playback policy.

Only the first responsive poster is prioritized. All four video readers use `preload="none"`; individual originals use `PublicVideoPlayer`. The pair and individual players pause each other, and at most one sound track is audible. Do not shorten the result to match the source's container duration or strip its audio tail. No YouTube embed, automatic import or fixed price is implied.

Focused verification is in `video-background-changer-route.test.ts`, `video-background-comparison.test.ts`, the shared playback/locale tests and the explicit Wan Prime case in `workspace-requested-workflow-dom.test.ts`. A real production-build browser recipe with qualified clips remains necessary for first Play, paused seek, range delivery and loading measurements; synthetic DOM tests do not establish those facts or real OAuth/password behavior.
